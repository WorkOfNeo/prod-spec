The shared list of every style awaiting review — and exactly how it's different from My tasks.

Open it from Reviews in the sidebar (it sits just under My tasks).
If you've wondered “isn't this the same as My tasks?”

They look alike because they use the same style cards — but they answer different questions. My tasks = “what's on me?” Reviews = “what's waiting for anyone?” This guide makes the difference concrete.

2 My tasks vs Reviews — the one real difference

Same cards, different scope. Here's the side-by-side.

📋 My tasks

  • Your slice of the work.
  • Sorted into buckets by your involvement: yours-unfinished, waiting for a first look, others', your rejections.
  • Answers: “What should I do next?”
  • Best as your daily starting point.

🗂️ Reviews

  • The whole queue — every awaiting style.
  • One flat list, no buckets, oldest first.
  • Answers: “What's waiting for anyone?”
  • Best when you've cleared your own and want to pick up the next thing.

3 When it's empty

If nothing is awaiting review, the queue says “Nothing awaiting review.” — “When a job finishes rendering, its style appears here for review.” That simply means nothing new has been generated yet; check back later or start your day on My tasks.

Rule of thumb. Start on My tasks to clear what's yours. Then hop to Reviews to pull the next waiting style. Same cards, same Approve/Reject screen behind them — just a wider net.
