Reviewing a style — the data

The style's own page: care instructions, the required fields, EAN barcodes & the PO PDF, and how approval flows back to Monday.

Open a style from Styles or a ← Back to style link. Guide 5 covered the Review tab (the documents); this covers the Details tab (the data behind them).

1 The style's tabs

A style page has a few tabs. You'll use two: Details (the data) and Review (decide on the documents). Everything below is on Details — where you check that the data behind the documents is right.

3 Required fields & readiness

A document can only be generated once the style's required fields are filled. The Details page shows you which are in and which are missing — the same “filled / total” you saw as Generation in the Styles list.

✗ Fibre composition   ✗ Supplier   ✗ Wash temp

Required vs the rest. Only the fields marked required (set under Settings ▸ Required fields) count toward readiness. Until enough are filled to cross the threshold, the style can't generate — its status stays Awaiting data.

What this means for you

If a document you need is missing or stuck, this is where you confirm why — a required field isn't filled. Flag it, or reject the document with a note, and it gets fixed and re-run.

5 The Monday link — it updates itself

Approval flows back to Monday automatically

You don't press anything to update Monday. The moment a style is fully approved and publishes, Prod Spec writes the result back to the Styles board on its own — flipping the label/packaging and box-marking sub-items to Approved and notifying the customer-responsible person.

It's best-effort: if Monday is briefly unreachable, your approval and the supplier hand-off still go through — the Monday update just catches up. So everything you approve here keeps the Monday board in step without any extra step from you.

How this ties together: the data on this page (care symbols, required fields, EAN/PO) is what produced the documents in guide 5. When something looks wrong on a document, this is where you confirm the cause — then reject with a note so the data gets fixed and re-run.
