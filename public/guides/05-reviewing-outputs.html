Reviewing a style — the outputs

What the Review screen shows you, what every status means, and exactly which buttons are yours. No technical knowledge needed.

You reach this screen by opening a card on My tasks or Reviews and pressing Start review / Finish review — or from a style's page via Review & decide.
Your two levers

On this screen you only ever do two things: ✓ Approve a document that's correct, or ✗ Reject one that isn't (with a short note saying why). You don't edit data or re-generate documents from here — if something's wrong, you reject it with a note, and a short how-to is further down.

The top of the screen tells you which style you're reviewing and, at a glance, how many of its documents are ready for you and where each one stands. This is your “X of Y outputs ready”.

6 Rejecting a document

Say what's wrong, in one box

When a document isn't right, press ✗ Reject… and type a short note. Your comment is saved to the rejection log with full context — customer, business area, order, and which document — so the underlying data can be fixed and the document re-run. The corrected version then comes back to you as a fresh review.

7 Finishing up — and not losing a half-done review

When you're confident, you can clear a whole style quickly. And the screen actively protects you from forgetting a review you've half-finished.

  1. 10Approve all / Reject all act on every pending document at once. Blocked ones are skipped automatically — you can't bulk-approve something that isn't print-safe.
  2. 11The moment the last document is decided and approved, the style is published: the documents are sent on to the supplier and the style's status flows back to Monday automatically. Until then, nothing leaves.
  3. 12If you wander off mid-review, the app reminds you and keeps the style waiting on your My tasks so it can't be forgotten (dialogs below).
