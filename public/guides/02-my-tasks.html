3 What the button is telling you

The button changes with the state of the style. Four you'll see:

Start review
Nobody's begun — you'll be the first. Marks the style as yours when you open it.
Finish review
You've decided some but not all documents. Pick up where you left off.
Blocked — fix & re-run
Remaining documents have placeholders (e.g. missing artwork / EAN) that must be fixed before they can be approved. Opens the style page.
Publish failed — retry
All approved, but the hand-off to the supplier hit a snag. Open the review screen and retry the send.

4 Starting a review & finishing the day

Click a card's button (or a Review → link) and you land on the Review screen — that's guide 5, where you approve and reject. Two things worth knowing:

It stays yours until it's done

Starting a review marks the style as in review by you. Even if you step away, it waits in ⚠ Unfinished reviews — yours so it can't be forgotten — and teammates see it's already in hand.

“All caught up.”

When there's nothing on you, the page says All caught up — no unfinished reviews, nothing waiting for a first look, no open rejections — with quick links to Browse styles, the Review queue, and Jobs.
