2 Reading a row

A few columns tell you, without opening anything, how far along a style is and whether it needs you.

ColumnWhat it tells you
StyleThe style number and name. Always shown.
PO / Customer / Business area / GroupWho and what it's for — the order number, the customer, and how it's grouped.
GenerationRequired fields filled, e.g. 5 / 8. Green when complete, amber when fields are still missing. Below the line it can't generate.
CompletionA bar: how much of the required data is filled, with a small tick marking the threshold a style must reach before it can generate.
StatusThe single most useful column — see below.
EANWhether the per-size barcodes have been read from the PO PDF (with a Resolve button to run it). More in guide 6.
Last syncedWhen the style last refreshed from Monday.

3 What the Status column means

Status reads differently depending on whether the documents exist yet. Two phases:

Before documents exist — is the data ready?

Awaiting data → Partially ready → Ready to generate

The style is gathering the fields it needs. Once it's “ready to generate,” the documents can be produced.

After documents exist — the review flow

Queued → Ready for review → Approved / Rejected

Ready for review is your cue — that style has documents waiting for you. It'll also be sitting in the Reviews queue.

Fastest path to your work: filter Status → Ready for review, or just use the Reviews queue. Use the Styles list when you need to find one specific style — by PO, customer or name — whatever state it's in.
