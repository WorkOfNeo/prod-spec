Prod Spec · Reviewer handbook

The reviewer's guide

Everything you do as a reviewer — finding styles, reading your tasks, and approving or rejecting the documents that get sent to suppliers. Written for non-technical users; no jargon.

· Your role in 30 seconds

Your job is straightforward: check the documents on a style and decide — approve what's correct, reject what isn't. Here's the shape of it.

✓ What you do here

  • + Browse every style and open its data
  • + Work from My tasks (what's on you) and the Reviews queue (everything waiting)
  • + Start a review so it's marked as yours
  • + Approve a document that's print-ready
  • + Reject a document with a note when something's wrong

· How a style reaches you

The same journey happens for every style. You come in at steps 3–4.

1Data syncs in from Monday — customer, order (PO), sizes, wash-care symbols and the rest land on the style automatically.
2The documents are generated — care labels, carton prints, hangtags, stickers… whatever the style's spec calls for.
3The finished documents arrive for review — they show up in the Reviews queue, and on your My tasks once you start one.
4You check each document and Approve it or Reject it with a note. This is your part.
5When every document is approved, the style publishes — the files go to the supplier and the status flows back to Monday automatically. Nothing leaves until then.
